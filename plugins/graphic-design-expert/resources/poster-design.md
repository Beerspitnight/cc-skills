# Poster & Marketing Design Guide

## Core Tenets
Posters must deliver messages to people on the go. Success is defined by the speed of communication from a distance.

## Standard Print Dimensions
### International (ISO)
- **A4:** 210 × 297mm (Standard Flyer)
- **A3:** 297 × 420mm (Small Poster)
- **A2:** 420 × 594mm (Medium Poster)
- **A1:** 594 × 841mm (Large/Retail)

### US Standards
- **Letter:** 8.5" × 11" (Flyer)
- **Tabloid:** 11" × 17" (Small Poster)
- **Architectural:** 18" × 24" (Medium)
- **Movie Poster:** 24" x 36" (Large)

## 10 Rules of Poster Design

1.  **Readable from a Distance**
    - **Hierarchy:** Headline (largest) > Details > Fine Print.
    - **Test:** Can the viewer understand the main subject from 10 feet away?

2.  **Contextual Contrast**
    - Determine where the poster will live (e.g., a white wall vs. a busy brick alley).
    - Design for contrast against the *environment*, not just within the artboard.

3.  **Audience Mirroring**
    - Match the visual voice to the demographic.
    - *Example:* A "Lion King" poster uses imagery of lions (nostalgia/recognition) rather than heavy text.

4.  **Strategic Typography & Color**
    - Use color to instill emotion (e.g., pink/pastels for soft vibes, red/yellow for energy).
    - Use bold fonts for impact; cursive/serifs for elegance.
    - Ensure high contrast between text and background.

5.  **Brand Consistency**
    - Even if experimental, the poster must use the brand's core font families and logo usage rules.

6.  **Call to Action (CTA)**
    - Unlike web, print CTAs are often subtle but must be present.
    - *Examples:* QR codes, "Tickets available at...", Date/Time/Location.

7.  **Dominant Focal Point**
    - Use ONE central image, icon, or headline.
    - Avoid clutter. If you emphasize everything, you emphasize nothing.

8.  **Exaggerated Spacing**
    - Use more whitespace than you think is necessary.
    - Icons and text need "breathing room" to be legible from a distance.

9.  **Shape & Size Dynamics**
    - Use shapes to guide the eye toward the focal point (e.g., leading lines).
    - Standard Sizes:
        - Large: 24" x 36" (Movie/Concert)
        - Medium: 18" x 24" (Advertisements)
        - Small: 11" x 17" (Flyers)

10. **Rule Breaking**
    - Once readability is assured, experiment with layout to create "stopping power."

## Pre-Print Checklist
- **Bleed:** Ensure artwork extends 3mm-5mm beyond the trim line.
- **Resolution:** 300 DPI minimum for print (vs. 72 DPI for web).
- **Color Mode:** Convert to CMYK (not RGB) before sending to the printer.
- **Paper Stock:** Decide on Glossy (vibrant colors) vs. Matte (no glare, easier to read).
- **Safe Zone:** Keep text 0.25" away from the edge.
