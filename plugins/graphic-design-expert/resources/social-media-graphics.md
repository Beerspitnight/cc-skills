# Social Media Design Strategy (2026)

## Core Philosophy
In 2026, a social graphic is a **"Micro Landing Page."** It is not just decoration; it is a product surface and public proof of operation.
- **Goal:** Distinctive, on-brand visuals at scale.
- **Metric:** Connection to business outcomes (pipeline, revenue), not just vanity engagement.

## The "Better Brief" Structure
To generate high-performing assets, every design request must contain:
1.  **Business Moment:** What is the specific outcome? (e.g., "Qualified Demo," not "General Awareness").
2.  **Constraint:** One message, one proof point.
3.  **Context:** 2-3 examples of past high-performing assets.
4.  **Guardrails:** Legal language and claims to avoid.

## Design Principles for High Performance
1.  **Hierarchy:** A clear focal point beats complex collages. The eye must settle immediately.
2.  **Typography:** Must signal category confidence. Legibility at small mobile sizes is non-negotiable.
3.  **Color:** Use color for *intent* (directing attention), not noise.
4.  **Motion:** Use motion to reveal information or guide the eye, not just for decoration.
5.  **Product Reality:** Product shots must feel "earned" and true to usage.

## AI-Native Workflow
Treat AI as a "Fast Junior Designer."
1.  **Explore:** Use AI to generate directions (e.g., "Lifestyle vs. UI Closeup").
2.  **Refine (Human):** Senior designers handle anatomy, composition, and edge cleanup.
3.  **Scale:** AI handles resizing and variations once the "Master" asset is approved.

## Governance & Safety
- **Rights:** Track licenses for all elements (fonts, stock, GenAI).
- **Provenance:** Attach content credentials to files.
- **Accessibility:**
  - Meet contrast targets.
  - Include Alt Text.
  - Ensure type size is legible on mobile screens.

## Localization
Global brands must treat localization as a core task.
- **AI Role:** Draft translations and layout adjustments.
- **Human Role:** Verify cultural nuances and idioms.
