# Color Psychology & Strategy

## 1. Warm Colors (Action & Emotion)
*High arousal. Best for food, sales, and impulse buys.*
- **Red:** Urgency, appetite, passion. (Fast food, Clearance sales).
- **Orange:** Value, affordability, creativity. (Budget brands, CTA buttons).
- **Yellow:** Optimism, caution. High visibility but causes eye strain.

## 2. Cool Colors (Trust & Calm)
*Low arousal. Best for B2B, finance, and health.*
- **Blue:** Trust, security, productivity. (Banks, Tech, Healthcare).
- **Green:** Wealth, health, tranquility. (Finance, Whole Foods).
- **Purple:** Luxury, royalty, creativity. (High-end services).

## 3. Neutrals (Structure)
- **Black:** Luxury, exclusivity, sophistication. (Fashion, Autos).
- **White:** Hygiene, clarity, simplicity. (Tech, Modern UI).
- **Brown:** Earthiness, reliability, organic. (Coffee, Sustainability).

## 4. Digital vs. Print
- **RGB:** Screens. Vibrant.
- **CMYK:** Print. Duller/Darker.
- **Warning:** Do not design neon brands in RGB if the main output is print.
