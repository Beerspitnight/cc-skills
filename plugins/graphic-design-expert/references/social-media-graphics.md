# Social Media Design Strategy (2026)

## Core Philosophy
A social graphic is a **"Micro Landing Page."**
- **Goal:** Distinctive, on-brand visuals at scale.
- **Metric:** Connection to business outcomes (pipeline, revenue), not just vanity engagement.

## The "Better Brief" Structure
1.  **Business Moment:** What is the specific outcome?
2.  **Constraint:** One message, one proof point.
3.  **Context:** 2-3 examples of past high-performing assets.

## Design Principles
1.  **Hierarchy:** A clear focal point beats complex collages.
2.  **Typography:** Must be readable at small mobile sizes.
3.  **Motion:** Use motion to reveal information, not just decoration.

## Audience Segmentation Strategy
When the target audience is diverse (e.g., "General Public"), do not try to mix every interest. Use **Behavioral Segmentation**:
- **Find the Common Thread:** (e.g., A "Foodie" and a "Business Exec" both appreciate *High Quality*).
- **Universal Drivers:**
  - **Psychology:** Blue conveys trust/calm; Orange conveys value/affordability.
  - **Visual Hierarchy:** If the audience is broad, the hierarchy must be simple.

## Governance
- **Rights:** Track licenses for all elements (fonts, stock, GenAI).
- **Accessibility:** Meet contrast targets and include Alt Text.
