# Poster & Print Design Guide

## 10 Rules of Poster Design
1.  **Readable from a Distance:** Test readability from 10 feet away.
2.  **Contextual Contrast:** Design for where the poster will hang (white wall vs brick).
3.  **Audience Mirroring:** Match visual voice to demographic.
4.  **Strategic Typography:** Use bold fonts for impact, serifs for elegance.
5.  **Brand Consistency:** Use core brand fonts/colors.
6.  **Call to Action:** Subtle but present (QR code, Date/Time).
7.  **Dominant Focal Point:** One central image/icon.
8.  **Exaggerated Spacing:** Icons need room to breathe.
9.  **Shape & Size:** Use lines to guide the eye.
10. **Rule Breaking:** Experiment once readability is assured.

## Pre-Print Checklist
- **Bleed:** 3mm-5mm beyond trim line.
- **Resolution:** 300 DPI minimum.
- **Color Mode:** CMYK.
- **Paper Stock:** Glossy vs. Matte.
