# Logo Design Taxonomy & Selection Guide

## 1. Wordmark (Logotype)
**Focus:** Typography-only, full company name.
- **Best For:** New businesses (helps name recognition), short names.
- **Examples:** Visa, Coca-Cola, Google.

## 2. Lettermark (Monogram)
**Focus:** Typography-only, initials/acronym.
- **Best For:** Long company names, difficult-to-pronounce names.
- **Examples:** NASA, HBO, IBM.

## 3. Symbol (Pictorial Mark)
**Focus:** Icon only (no text).
- **Best For:** Established global brands, apps (favicons).
- **Examples:** Apple, Twitter bird, Target bullseye.

## 4. Abstract Mark
**Focus:** Geometric form, conceptual, non-literal.
- **Best For:** Tech companies, SaaS, "invisible" products.
- **Examples:** Pepsi, Adidas, Slack.

## 5. Mascot
**Focus:** Illustrated character.
- **Best For:** Family-friendly brands, food, sports teams.
- **Examples:** KFC, Mailchimp.

## 6. Emblem
**Focus:** Text inside a symbol (Badge/Crest).
- **Best For:** Auto industry, Universities, Government.
- **Examples:** Starbucks, Harley-Davidson, NFL.

## 7. Combination Mark
**Focus:** Text + Symbol.
- **Best For:** Most small businesses. Versatile.
- **Examples:** Burger King, Doritos.

## Selection Logic
- **Name is long (3+ words)?** -> Lettermark.
- **Industry is Tech/SaaS?** -> Abstract.
- **Industry is Food/Retail?** -> Mascot or Combination.
- **Need Trust/Heritage?** -> Emblem or Serif Wordmark.
