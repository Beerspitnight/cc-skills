# Evaluation & Analysis Framework

## 1. Unit Economics Validation
Before accepting the user's numbers, check for these red flags. If found, highlight them in the **Unit Economics Model** section.

*   **LTV:CAC Ratio**: 
    *   *Formula*: `((ARPU - COGS) / Churn) / CAC`
    *   *Benchmark*: Ideal is > 3:1. If < 1:1, the business is not viable.
*   **Payback Period**:
    *   *Formula*: `CAC / (ARPU - COGS)`
    *   *Benchmark*: SMB/Mid-market should be < 12 months. Enterprise < 18 months.
*   **Gross Margin**:
    *   *Formula*: `(Revenue - COGS) / Revenue`
    *   *Benchmark*: Software should be > 70%. Services > 30%. E-commerce > 30%.

## 2. GTM Strategy Critique
Evaluate the channel fit based on the ARPU (Average Revenue Per User).

*   **Low ARPU (<$100/yr)**: Requires viral loops, SEO, or organic social. Paid ads (CAC) usually break the model here.
*   **Mid ARPU ($1k-$5k/yr)**: Can support content marketing, inside sales, and efficient paid ads.
*   **High ARPU ($50k+/yr)**: Requires field sales, ABM (Account Based Marketing), and long sales cycles.

*Flag Mismatch*: If a user proposes "Direct Sales Force" for a $5/month app, flag this as a critical GTM risk.

## 3. Risk Categories
When populating the **Risk & Mitigation Plan**, categorize risks into:

*   **Market Risk**: "Does anyone actually want this?" (Mitigation: Presales, Waitlists).
*   **Product Risk**: "Can we actually build this?" (Mitigation: Prototyping, phased rollouts).
*   **Execution/Financial Risk**: "Will we run out of money?" (Mitigation: Lower burn rate, staged fundraising).

## 4. Market Sizing (TAM/SAM/SOM) definitions
*   **TAM (Total Addressable Market)**: Total market demand for a product/service.
*   **SAM (Serviceable Available Market)**: The segment of the TAM targeted by your products and services which is within your geographical reach.
*   **SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market)**: The portion of SAM that you can capture.
