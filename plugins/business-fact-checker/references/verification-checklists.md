# Verification Checklists by Claim Type

## 1. Financial Metrics (Revenue, Profit, Margins)
- [ ] **Define the Metric**: Is it GAAP Revenue or "Bookings"? Net Income or "Adjusted EBITDA"?
- [ ] **Check Timeframe**: Is it LTM (Last Twelve Months), FY (Fiscal Year), or Annualized Run Rate?
- [ ] **Currency Check**: Verify exchange rates if consolidated globally.
- [ ] **Source Trace**: Locate the number in a 10-Q/10-K or audited statement.
- [ ] **Context**: Compare YoY (Year over Year) and QoQ (Quarter over Quarter) to detect cherry-picking.

## 2. Valuations & Funding
- [ ] **Pre vs. Post**: Is the valuation Pre-Money or Post-Money?
- [ ] **Equity vs. Enterprise**: Is it Market Cap (Equity Value) or Enterprise Value (EV)?
- [ ] **Date of Valuation**: Valuations expire quickly. Is this from the last round (months ago) or current secondary markets?
- [ ] **Source**: Confirmed by PitchBook/SEC filing, or just a "rumored" TechCrunch article?

## 3. Market Sizing (TAM/SAM/SOM)
- [ ] **Source Date**: Market reports older than 2 years are usually obsolete in tech.
- [ ] **Geographic Scope**: Does "Global Market" match the company's "US-only" operations?
- [ ] **Definition**: Does the report define the market the same way the company does?
- [ ] **Origin**: Is the report from a reputable firm (Gartner) or a "pay-for-play" research mill?

## 4. "First/Best/Only" Claims
- [ ] **Patent Search**: If they claim "proprietary tech," does a patent exist?
- [ ] **Competitor Scan**: Search for `[Competitor] vs [Product]` to find existing alternatives.
- [ ] **Review Aggregators**: Check G2, Capterra, or Trustpilot for user validation of "Best" claims.
