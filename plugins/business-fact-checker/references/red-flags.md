# Business Misinformation Indicators & Red Flags

## Quantitative Manipulation
*   **"Community Adjusted EBITDA"**: Invented non-GAAP metrics that exclude real costs (e.g., ignoring stock-based compensation or marketing).
*   **Cumulative vs. Annual**: Graphing "Cumulative Revenue" to show a curve going up when annual revenue is flat or declining.
*   **Bar Chart Truncation**: Starting the Y-axis at a non-zero number to exaggerate small growth.
*   **Mismatching Units**: Comparing a competitor's *Net Income* to the company's *Gross Revenue*.

## Qualitative/Rhetorical Manipulation
*   **"In Talks" / "Planning to"**: Treating future possibilities as current partnerships.
*   **Name Dropping**: "Used by employees at Google" (meaning 1 person used the free tier) vs "Enterprise contract with Google."
*   **Circular Sourcing**: A startup cites a blog, which cites the startup's own press release.
*   **False Urgency**: "Oversubscribed round closing in 24h" (common in high-pressure sales/fundraising).

## Source Credibility Issues
*   **Sponsored Content**: Articles labeled "Brand Partner" or "Sponsored" presented as objective news.
*   **Zombie Firms**: Research firms that haven't published in 3+ years.
*   **Anonymous "Insiders"**: Be skeptical of M&A rumors relying solely on anonymous sources without secondary corroboration.
