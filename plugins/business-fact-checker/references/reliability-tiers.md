# Business Evidence Reliability Tiers

When verifying business claims, prioritize sources in this order. A claim supported only by Tier 4 or 5 is **UNVERIFIED**.

## Tier 1: Primary & Regulatory (The Gold Standard)
*   **Regulatory Filings**: SEC (EDGAR), Companies House (UK), SEDAR (Canada).
    *   *Docs*: 10-K, 10-Q, 8-K, S-1, Proxy Statements.
*   **Government Statistics**: Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS), Census Bureau, Federal Reserve Data (FRED).
*   **Legal Records**: Court dockets (PACER), Patent filings (USPTO/WIPO).
*   **Direct Transaction Data**: Confirmed blockchain txs (for crypto), deed records.

## Tier 2: Institutional & Top-Tier Media (High Credibility)
*   **Major Financial Press**: Wall Street Journal, Financial Times, Bloomberg, Reuters.
    *   *Note*: Distinguish between *News* (High) and *Opinion/Contributors* (Medium).
*   **Equity Research**: Analyst reports from major banks (Goldman, JPM, Morgan Stanley) - specific notes, not general summaries.
*   **Market Intelligence Firms**: IDC, Gartner, Forrester, PitchBook, Crunchbase (Data verified by analysts).

## Tier 3: Industry Trade & Company Disclosures (Medium Credibility)
*   **Trade Publications**: TechCrunch, AdAge, The Information, industry-specific verticals.
*   **Company Disclosures**: Press Releases, Investor Presentations, Earnings Call Transcripts.
    *   *Warning*: These are marketing documents. Numbers are often "adjusted" or framed favorably.
*   **Whitepapers**: Technical papers released by the company.

## Tier 4: Aggregators & Opinion (Low Credibility)
*   **News Aggregators**: Yahoo Finance (automated feeds), Google Finance.
*   **General News**: CNN, BBC, NYT (general desk, not business desk).
*   **Wikipedia**: Good for finding sources, bad as a source itself.

## Tier 5: Unverified & Social (Use with Extreme Caution)
*   **Social Media**: LinkedIn posts, Twitter/X threads, Reddit (r/investing, r/wallstreetbets).
*   **Blogs/Newsletters**: Substack, Medium (unless authored by a verified Tier 1 expert).
*   **"Content Farms"**: SEO-driven financial advice sites.
