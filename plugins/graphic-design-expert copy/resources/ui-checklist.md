# Graphic Design Quality Checks

## 1. Visual Polish
- **Alignment:** All elements aligned to the grid; nothing drifting.
- **Spacing Consistency:** Margin/padding follows a predictable scale (4, 8, 12, etc.).
- **Balance & Composition:** No lopsided visual weight or awkward negative space.
- **Shadows:** Consistent light source, intensity, and softness.
- **Edges & Masking:** Crisp edges, clean masks, no accidental halos.
- **Color Cleanup:** No unnecessary near-duplicate shades; palette stays intentional.


## 2. Component Structure (Atomic Design)
When organizing UI, categorize elements by complexity:
- **Atoms:** Buttons, Inputs, Icons, Labels (Indivisible).
- **Molecules:** Search Bar (Input + Button), Form Field (Label + Input).
- **Organisms:** Navigation Bar, Product Card, Footer.
- **Templates:** Wireframe structure of a page.

## 3. Typography
- **Hierarchy Clarity:** H1, H2, H3, and Body text feel distinct and structured.
- **Readability:** Strong contrast and legible sizes across devices.
- **Type Spacing:** Line height, tracking, and paragraph spacing are consistent and deliberate.
- **Line Length:** 45-75 characters per line.
- **Font Pairing:** Only brand-approved fonts used; pairings consistent across assets.
- **Widows/Orphans:** Avoid single-word stragglers at the end or beginning of lines/paragraphs.

## 4. Color & Accessibility
- **Contrast Ratios:** Meets accessibility standards for text, icons, and UI.
- **Brand Colors:** Exact color values used—no "almost matching" hex codes.
- **Color Meaning:** Visual intent matches established conventions (e.g., red = error, green = success).

## 5. Interaction & States
- **States:** Hover, Active, Focus, Disabled, Error, Success.
- **Feedback:** Loading skeletons or spinners for async actions.
- **Touch Targets:** Minimum 44x44px for mobile.

## 6. Functionality & UI Logic
- **Interaction States:** Hover, active, focus, and disabled states included and consistent.
- **Feedback:** Success, error, loading, and empty states accounted for.
- **Touch Targets:** Buttons and interactive elements sized appropriately for touch.
- **Icon Behavior:** Icons behave consistently; no accidental animations.

## 7. Branding & Identity
- **Logo Use:** Correct logo, proportions, and clear space.
- **Tone & Voice Alignment:** Visual style supports the brand's personality.
- **Cross-Asset Consistency:** This piece matches the broader brand system.

## 8. Content Accuracy
- **Spelling & Grammar:** No typos or punctuation slip-ups.
- **Key Details:** Dates, URLs, product names, and prices double-checked.
- **Information Hierarchy:** Most important content is the most visually prominent.

## 9. Technical & Export
- **Correct Dimensions:** Artboard size matches platform or print spec.
- **Resolution:** Print = 300dpi; digital = 72/144dpi as appropriate.
- **Compression:** Optimized exports without visible degradation.
- **File Organization:** Properly named layers and folders.
- **Bleeds & Safe Zones:** Included for print; respected for digital assets.

## 10. Final Sanity Pass
- **Zoom Test:** Looks clean at 100%, 50%, and 200%.
- **Distance Test:** Legible and clear when viewed from far away.
- **Black & White Test (Optional):** Still communicates without color.
- **Instinct Check:** If something feels wrong, fix it now—not later.
